About

An independent trading operation.

BYT trades exchange-listed derivatives using capital it owns. There are no outside investors, no managed accounts, and no advisory services — the firm is founder-led and operates entirely in-house, from research through execution.

That independence keeps incentives simple. Every strategy is judged on process and risk-adjusted results — not on asset-gathering, not on client relationships, and not on a story that has to sound good to outside investors.

Day to day, that means building and maintaining its own tooling — data collection, backtesting infrastructure, and monitoring — rather than relying on a vendor stack or an off-the-shelf platform.

No outside capital. No client relationships. Just process.
